**The history reports are kept as they were written.** They cite paths from before the 2026-10-24 consolidation:
* `reports/<name>.json` is now `reports/evidence/<name>.json`;
* the round reports are now in `reports/history/`;
* the analysis scripts are now in `scripts/analysis/`;
* the original notebooks are now in `archive/`.

Each evidence file is regenerated by the script named in `scripts/README.md`. Before and after the consolidation, all of them were regenerated from the stored predictions and compared with the committed versions.

`runs/` (git-ignored) holds Round 1 per-run metrics written by `scripts/evaluate.py`. `challenge_v2/` will hold the ledger of independent-benchmark evaluations.

# Datasets and splits: inventory for reviewers

This page lists every dataset the project used, what it was used for, and where its identity is recorded. It adds nothing new to the data. Files, paths and split membership are unchanged, and no dataset is copied here. Detailed lineage is in `docs/DATA_PIPELINE.md` §0; the per-run training data is in the training summaries.

Counts are **verified** against the files named in the last column (split id files, manifests, training summaries, seal) on 2026-10-26. Counts marked "(reported)" come from a report or notebook output and were not re-counted.

## 1. At a glance: training, validation and test

| ML role | Dataset(s) in this project | Documents | May it influence decisions? |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Training** | original `train` split, plus format-A augmented copies, plus `B_train` (final model R3) | 49,685 | — |
| **Validation** (epoch selection; rule tuning) | original `validation` split | 4,350 | yes |
| **Model selection** between candidate systems | `dev_v1` (hand-written) | 135 | yes, used once (Round 2) |
| **Development testing and regression** | original `test` split and its slices, `test_format_shift`, `test_long`, `validation_long`, `synth_A_heldout`, `synth_B_heldout`, `challenge_v1` (+ overlays) | see §3 | never tuned on, but several served as pass/fail gates or were analysed in depth (§4) |
| **Final independent evaluation** | `challenge_v2` (hidden, independently authored) | 400 | **no**: evaluated once per mode after the freeze |

**The original `test` split is not an untouched final test.** It was never used for training or epoch selection, but it was reported in every round, served as a non-inferiority gate in the Round 2 selection rule, was the source of `test_format_shift` and `test_long` (both gates or analysis sets), and one early rules version's per-class output on it was looked at once (`reports/history/INVESTIGATION.md` §3). It is a **development test set**. The only final, independent test is `challenge_v2`.

## 3. Relationship between original and generated data

* **Original:** the 43,501 English ai4privacy records and their three splits. Labels come from the source and are corrected by the versioned label policy (`labels.py`: v1 original notebooks, v2 Round 1, v3 Round 2 and final).
* **Derived from the original** (same licence restriction): format-A training copies (from `train`), `test_format_shift` (format family B, from `test`), `test_long` and `validation_long` (concatenations), and the `test_*` slices (subsets of `test`).
* **Generated by the project** (no third-party text): `A_train`, `B_train`, `synth_A_heldout`, `synth_B_heldout`. Split by template family: 11 of 48 families held out. A contamination guard drops documents similar to `dev_v1`/`challenge_v1` (maximum similarity 0.20) or reusing their values.
* **Hand-written** (fictional values): `dev_v1`, `challenge_v1` and overlays, all by the development side.
* **Independent**: `challenge_v2` only.

## 5. Rebuilding

```bash
python scripts/build_synth_round2.py                               # data/synth/round2 (deterministic)
python scripts/build_eval_sets.py --policy v3 --out data/eval_v3   # needs data/raw/english_pii_43k.jsonl
```

Both need the raw file, downloaded as described in `docs/DATA_PIPELINE.md` §0. `.gitattributes` keeps `data/**` byte-exact, because the hashes are recorded in manifests and prediction run files. Do not re-run `archive/02_mbert_dataset_splitting.ipynb` or `03_…` in place: they write to `data/processed/` (`archive/README.md`).
